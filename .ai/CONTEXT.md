# Vilo App Domain Context & Feature Map

## App Concept
**Vilo** is a high-performance short-form content platform (similar to TikTok / Instagram Reels) with an integrated e-commerce shopping experience (product tagging on videos, showcase store, in-app cart and checkout), intentionally excluding live video broadcasting.

---

## Feature Modules

1. **`feed/`**: Vertical video feed (For You, Following), gesture interactions, video overlays (author, caption, audio disc, shop product pill), comment bottom sheets.
2. **`shop/`**: Integrated store catalog, product detail pages with variant picker, reviews, cart modal, and checkout flow.
3. **`creator/`**: Video creation studio (Camera preview, recording, trimming, filters, audio sync, and shop product tagging).
4. **`profile/`**: User & creator profiles, video grid tabs (uploads, likes, bookmarks, showcase shop), and "People You May Know" suggestions.
5. **`search_discover/`**: Universal search, 3-column trending shortform video grid, featured accounts, and trending audio/sounds.
6. **`inbox_activity/`**: Activity notifications (likes, mentions, orders) and direct chats.
7. **`bottom_nav/`**: Floating Liquid Glass navigation dock with 5-phase spring physics and Instagram-style scroll-driven compact morphing.
